How records work

  • Origin: where and when the item was made, and by whom if known.
  • Ownership history: the owners we can document, with dates and sources.
  • Condition: repairs, restoration and wear, described by an inspector.
  • Authenticity: the checks carried out and who carried them out.

Gaps in a record are shown as gaps. We do not fill them in.
